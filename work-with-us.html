How It Actually Works.

Four simple steps. No training course, no fancy CRM, just a phone and some hustle.

01

Find Businesses

Open Google or Google Maps and search things like "restaurants in [your city]", "clinics near me", or "boutiques in [area]". Look for shops, doctors, gyms, salons, schools, or any local business with no website, or an old, broken-looking one. Note down the business name and phone number.

02

Make the Call

Call them directly. Introduce yourself, say you work with BuyMeWebsite.com, and ask if they have two minutes to hear how a website could bring them more customers. Keep it short, friendly, and genuinely helpful, not pushy.

03

Pitch the Value

Explain why BuyMeWebsite is a better option than anyone else they've considered, half the market price, domain and hosting included, and a real team behind it. Everything you need to say is laid out below.

04

Close & Get Paid

If they're interested, connect them to us on WhatsApp or bring us into the call. Once they confirm and pay their advance, the deal is yours, and so is your commission. Simple as that.

Questions? Answered.

Do I need any experience to start?

No. If you can make a phone call and hold a friendly conversation, you can do this. We give you everything you need to say, you don't need a sales background.

Do I need to invest any money?

Zero. This is entirely commission based. You're not buying a kit, a course, or a license. You bring the calls, we build the websites.

When and how do I get paid?

Your commission is paid out once the client you brought in confirms the project and pays their advance. Payment is sent directly to you via UPI or bank transfer.

Is there a minimum target I need to hit?

No fixed target. This is flexible, do it part time, full time, or just a few calls a week. Your commission tier simply reflects how many sites you close in a month.

What exactly do I need to do on the call?

Introduce yourself, ask if they have a website (or if they're happy with their current one), and walk them through why BuyMeWebsite is worth considering, using the pitch points above. If they're interested, hand them off to us and we'll take it from there.

Can I do this alongside my job or studies?

Yes. Most people do this in their free time, a few calls in the evening or on weekends is enough to start seeing results.
