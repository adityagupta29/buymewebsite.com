FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How can you offer such low prices?

We're a small, remote-first team with low overhead. No agency middlemen, no fancy offices. We pass those savings directly to you. Quality is never compromised. We just cut the fluff.

Is domain and hosting really included?

Yes. Every package includes a free domain (.com / .in / .org), hosting, and SSL for 1 full year (approx. ₹5,000 onwards value). After 1 year, you renew at standard market rates or move to any provider you choose, no lock-in.

How does the payment plan work?

You pay only 10% advance to start. The remaining amount can be split into weekly instalments or milestone-based parts, like an EMI. You always see progress before paying the next chunk.

Do you build websites for schools, restaurants, doctors, lawyers, and other businesses?

Yes. We build affordable websites for every kind of business, schools, colleges, companies, restaurants, doctors, lawyers, real estate agents, and NGOs across India. Every industry gets the same transparent pricing and 1-year domain + hosting.

Can I buy a website online without meeting anyone in person?

Absolutely. The entire process, from discovery call to design approval to launch, happens over WhatsApp, call, and email. You can get your website made from anywhere in India without a single in-person meeting.

How long does delivery take?

Portfolio sites: 3-5 days. Branding sites: 7-10 days. CMS: 2-3 weeks. E-commerce: 3-4 weeks. Complex tools: 4-8 weeks depending on scope.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes 3-6 months of free support depending on the package. After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance plans starting at ₹1,500/month.

What if I don't like the design?

We iterate until you're happy. The design phase includes unlimited revisions. We don't move to development until you approve the mockup.
